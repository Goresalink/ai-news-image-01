# World News — Friday 13 November 2026 (news-image-01)

One image summing up the top world news of Friday 13 November 2026: a
newspaper-style infographic (1024×1024 PNG) with eight labeled story panels.

## The eight stories

1. **Ukraine peace talks** — Europe joins the next round; Putin restates full
   terms after meeting US envoys Witkoff & Kushner; the largest prisoner
   exchange of the war is being prepared.
2. **Nvidia CEO resigns** — Jensen Huang steps down; CFO Colette Kress becomes
   interim CEO; shares fall about 16%.
3. **Microsoft 365 outage** — Outlook and Teams down worldwide.
4. **Iran–US tensions** — Revolutionary Guards claim destroying a US radar in
   Bahrain; four oil tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz; Trump says the US
   needs no Iran deal.
5. **Khan Younis strike** — Israeli strike on Nasser hospital kills six,
   including a surgeon.
6. **US Senate** — Chris Van Hollen challenges Chuck Schumer for Democratic
   leadership.
7. **Binance hack seizure** — US DOJ wins court approval to seize $1.2B,
   blamed on North Korea.
8. **Also today** — Pope Leo meets abuse survivors in Peru; a Brooklyn
   building collapse kills eight; Storm Charles hits Portugal.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies the bundled `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte.
Python 3 standard library only; no network access. Running it twice produces
identical output bytes.
